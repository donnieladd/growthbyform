HANDOFF item 5 (`docs/handoff/05-pricing-research.md`, backlog `05271c23`). Research only — **no
pricing decision is made here**; this is the input to that decision, reserved for the owner.

Researched 2026-09-17 via each vendor's own pricing pages where published, cross-checked against
third-party aggregators (Capterra, ChurchMemberPro, TrustRadius) where a vendor doesn't publish
numbers. Every figure below is dated and sourced — treat anything not carrying a source link as
unconfirmed.

## Per-vendor detail

### Planning Center

Free-forever entry point (People has no paywall at all), then each app — Services, Check-Ins,
Giving, Groups, Calendar, Registrations, Publishing — is its own subscription on a shared price
ladder once usage crosses a free threshold. A typical 150-person church running Services,
Check-Ins, Giving, Groups, and Calendar together lands around $160–180/mo per third-party
estimates; Planning Center's own site doesn't publish a single number, it uses an interactive
calculator. Texting is a clearly separate paid add-on ($5/mo + $0.02/text) — not bundled into any
core app's price.

Sources: [Planning Center Pricing](https://www.planningcenter.com/pricing) ·
[Planning Center Pricing Help Doc](https://help.planningcenter.com/en/136790-planning-center-pricing.html) ·
[Planning Center Pricing Guide — ChurchMemberPro](https://churchmemberpro.com/blog/planning-center-pricing-guide/)

### Church Community Builder (CCB) / Pushpay

CCB's own domain (`churchcommunitybuilder.com/pricing`) now 301-redirects to `pushpay.com` — the
two are commercially the same company today, though Pushpay's site doesn't itself acknowledge the
CCB name. Pricing is fully gated behind "request a demo" / sales contact on both properties; no
dollar figure appears anywhere on either site. Third-party estimates cluster around $145–150/mo
entry, scaling to $500–1,500+/mo once bundled with Pushpay's giving product for larger churches —
treat these as community estimates, not confirmed figures.

Sources: [Pushpay](https://pushpay.com/) · [Pushpay Pricing](https://pushpay.com/product/pricing/) ·
[CCB Review — ChurchMemberPro](https://churchmemberpro.com/blog/ccb-church-community-builder-review/)

### Breeze ChMS

The simplest pricing story in the set: one flat rate, $72/mo, no tiers based on size or contact
count, no setup fee, no contract, 30-day free trial. Unlimited contacts, email blasts, check-in,
volunteer scheduling, and service planning all included at that one price. Texting is included up
to 250/mo, then $10 per additional 500. Breeze cross-bundles with Tithe.ly's products (see below) —
"Bundle Breeze ChMS with Breeze Worship Tools, Tithely Church Apps, and Tithely Sites" for $119/mo —
which strongly suggests a commercial relationship between the two brands.

Source: [Breeze ChMS Pricing](https://www.breezechms.com/pricing)

### Rock RMS

Not a vendor in the normal sense — a nonprofit, open-source project (Spark Development Network).
The software itself has no license fee; churches are asked to donate $4.45 per average weekend
attendee per year on an honor system (a 100-person church → ~$445/yr suggested, voluntary, not
enforced). Real cost lives in hosting and technical operation: self-host for free if you have the
infrastructure and staff, or use Rock Cloud / Cloud 9 through a hosting partner at roughly
$50–200+/mo depending on the partner — no messaging-cost information was published. This is the
one product here that's genuinely build-it-yourself: most configurable, least turnkey, and it
assumes a technical team on staff or a paid partner.

Source: [Rock RMS Pricing](https://www.rockrms.com/pricing)

### Tithe.ly

Church Management is $72/mo standalone (unlimited admins, volunteer coordination, email editor,
250 texts/mo included) — the same $72 entry price as Breeze, and Tithe.ly's "All Access" bundle
explicitly folds in Breeze-adjacent products, reinforcing that these two brands are commercially
linked. All Access lists at $228/mo but is offered at $119/mo, bundling giving, text giving ($19
standalone), church management ($72 standalone), worship planning ($29 standalone), a custom app
($89 standalone), and a custom website ($19 standalone). Enterprise is custom-quoted with a
dedicated success manager.

Source: [Tithely Pricing](https://get.tithe.ly/pricing)

### ChurchTrac

Priced strictly by "names tracked": $29/mo at 75 names up to $119/mo unlimited, with a 6-step
ladder in between (125/$39, 250/$59, 500/$79, 1000/$99). Every tier gets the same feature set —
people, accounting, giving, check-in, worship planning, groups, volunteers, basic messaging,
registrations, automations. Two-way texting and group messaging are NOT in the base price — that's
a separate "Enhanced Messaging" add-on at $7/mo for 250 credits. Paying annually saves 10%. Older
web results describe a genuine free tier under 100 people; the current live pricing page no longer
shows one, just a 30-day trial — noting the discrepancy rather than resolving it, since pricing
pages change and this research reflects what's live today.

Source: [ChurchTrac Pricing](https://www.churchtrac.com/pricing)

### Subsplash

Giving is free to run (Subsplash earns on processing: ~2.3%+$0.30 card, ~1% ACH). Everything else —
website, custom mobile app, church management — sits behind "Subsplash One" or "Enterprise," both
custom-quoted with no published number; third-party estimates put the full platform around
$99–200+/mo. Of everything researched, Subsplash's product story leans hardest toward the
member-facing app experience rather than staff-side pipeline/admin tooling.

Sources: [Subsplash Pricing](https://www.subsplash.com/pricing) ·
[Subsplash Pricing 2026 — Buildify](https://usebuildify.com/post/subsplash-pricing-2026-breakdown)

## What this implies for us

*(The researcher's observation, not a decision — pricing itself is the owner's call per the brief.)*

- **None of these products are pipeline-first.** Every one of them is a general-purpose church
  database (people, giving, check-in) with workflow/automation bolted on if at all. Nobody in this
  set organizes around "where is this person, and what does the church owe them next" the way
  Growth Track's spine does. That's a real positioning gap, not just a feature checklist gap.
- **Messaging is almost universally a separate line item**, not part of the core price — Planning
  Center, ChurchTrac, and Tithe.ly's standalone text-giving all charge for it distinctly, and
  Breeze/Tithe.ly cap a free allowance before charging per-block. Growth Track's stated design —
  one subscription, SMS/email metered in as a cost input rather than sold as an add-on — is a
  genuine structural difference from every product researched here, not just a friendlier price.
- **Two vendors (CCB/Pushpay, Subsplash's full platform) hide all pricing behind a sales call.**
  Breeze and ChurchTrac are the most transparent (published numbers, no contract). If self-serve
  signup without a sales call is part of the go-to-market thesis, Breeze/ChurchTrac/Planning
  Center's transparency is the closer comparison set, not CCB/Pushpay/Subsplash's.
- **Entry price across the transparent vendors clusters in a fairly narrow band** — roughly
  $29–$119/mo for a small-to-mid church across ChurchTrac, Breeze, Tithe.ly, and low-usage Planning
  Center — which at least brackets where a comparably-positioned product would need to sit to not
  look wildly out of market, independent of what it's actually worth.
- **Rock RMS is the outlier worth naming**: free software, real cost in hosting + technical staff.
  Not a direct pricing comparison so much as a reminder that "free" software still has a real,
  non-zero cost of ownership — worth keeping in mind if "free tier" ever comes up in our own
  pricing discussion.
